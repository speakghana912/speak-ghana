Nkɔmmɔdie ne Nsɛmmisa

Chapter 4 · Dialogues — Time, Days, Family and Numbers in Real Conversation

Role-play time!

Practice these five dialogues aloud — with a partner, a family member, or by recording yourself.

Each dialogue uses what you learned in this chapter: numbers, age, time, days of the week, family vocabulary, adjectives, and question patterns. Read both parts, then switch roles.

1

Ahyiaa Foforɔ

Meeting Someone New
A: Akwaaba! Wo din de sɛn?Welcome! What is your name?
B: Me din de Kofi. Na wo nso?My name is Kofi. And you?
A: Me din de Ama. Wote he?My name is Ama. Where do you live?
B: Mete Accra. Na wo nso?I live in Accra. And you?
A: Mefiri Kumasi, nanso mete Accra nnɛ.I am from Kumasi, but I live in Accra now.
B: Woadi mfeɛ sɛn?How old are you?
A: Mewɔ mfeɛ aduonu nkron. Na wo nso?I am 29 years old. And you?
B: Mewɔ mfeɛ aduasa baako. Woyɛ dɛn adwuma?I am 31. What work do you do?
A: Meyɛ ɔkyerɛkyerɛni. Na wo nso?I am a teacher. And you?
B: Meyɛ dwadini. Metɔn nwoma wɔ gua.I am a trader. I sell books at the market.
A: Ɛyɛ papa! Ɛyɛ anigye sɛ mahyia wo.That is great! It is a pleasure to meet you.
B: Me nso, anigye ne me.For me too, it is a pleasure.
2

Nhyehyɛeɛ Yɛ

Making Plans
A: Berɛ sɛn nnɛ?What time is it now?
B: Ɛyɛ berɛ nnum ne fa.It is half past five.
A: Yɛbɛhyia anwummerɛ?Shall we meet in the evening?
B: Ɛyɛ. Berɛ sɛn?Yes. What time?
A: Mepɛ sɛ yɛhyia nnɔnwɔtwe.I would like us to meet at eight o'clock.
B: Ɛyɛ me. He na yɛbɛhyia?That works for me. Where shall we meet?
A: Yɛbɛhyia wɔ gua no ho.We will meet near the market.
B: Dɛn na yɛbɛyɛ?What shall we do?
A: Yɛbɛdi biribi, na yɛnkɔ film.We will eat something, then go to the cinema.
B: Ɛyɛ dɛ! Mekɔ hwɛ kwan.That sounds good!
3

Abusua Ho Nkɔmmɔ

Talking About Family
A: Hwan na ɔwɔ wo fie?Who is at your home?
B: Me ne me papa, me maame, ne me nua barima na ɛte fie.My father, mother, and brother live at home with me.
A: Wo wɔ nuanom ahe?How many siblings do you have?
B: Mewɔ nuanom baasa. Nua barima baako ne nua bɛmea mmienu. Na wo nso?I have three siblings. One brother and two sisters. And you?
A: Me nko ara. Meni nuanom.I am alone. I have no siblings.
B: Wo papa ne wo maame te he?Where do your father and mother live?
A: Wɔn nyinaa te Kumasi. Wɔn yɛ adwuma wɔ hɔ.They both live in Kumasi. They work there.
B: Wo papa yɛ dɛn?What does your father do?
A: Ɔyɛ ɔkyerɛkyerɛni ɔpanyin wɔ sukuu mu.He is a senior teacher at the school.
B: Ne maame nso?And his mother?
A: Ɔyɛ nɛɛse wɔ ayaresabea.She is a nurse at the hospital.
B: Ɛyɛ papa adwuma!Those are great jobs!
4

Wɔ Edwam

At the Market
A: Maakye! Ɛte sɛn?Good morning! How is it?
B: Maakye! Ɛyɛ asomdwee. Hwɛ dɛn na wopɛ?Good morning! It is peaceful. What are you looking for?
A: Mepɛ aburoo fɛfɛ. Ɛyɛ sɛn?I want nice oranges. How much are they?
B: Aburoo no, baako yɛ sika aduonu.Each orange is 20 cedis.
A: Ɛyɛ dɔ! Te boɔ ase kakra.That is expensive! Reduce the price a little.
B: Eye, mede aduonu baako bɛma wo.Okay, I will give it to you for 21 cedis.
A: Mepɛ du. Ɛyɛ sɛn nyinaa?I want ten. How much altogether?
B: Du no, ɛyɛ sika ɔha aduonu.Ten of them is 120 cedis.
A: Mede sika ɔha bɛma wo.I will give you 100 cedis.
B: Yoo, mate ase. Fa wo aburoo.Okay, understood. Take your oranges.
A: Medaase paa!Thank you very much!
B: Yɛnda ase. Bra bio!No problem. Come again!
5

Kwan Kyerɛ

Asking for Directions
A: Mepa wo kyɛw, mehwehwɛ gua no.Please, I am looking for the market.
B: Gua no? Ɛwɔ hɔ a ɛbɛn.The market? It is close by.
A: Ɛhe? Merim hɔ.Where exactly? I am lost.
B: Fa saa kwan yi kɔ anim. Bere a wohunu asɔre no, dan kɔ nifa.Take this road forward. When you see the church, turn right.
A: Ɛyɛ ntɛm?Is it far?
B: Daabi. Ɛyɛ nantew nnum.No. It is a five-minute walk.
A: Abɔ sɛn nnɛ?What time is it now?
B: Abɔ nnɔnsia ne fa. Gua no da kyeɛ.It is half past six. The market is still open.
A: Medaase pa ara!Thank you very much!
B: Nante yie!Walk well!
Practice tip: Pick Dialogue 1 or 3 and change the details to your own real life — your actual age, your real family, where you actually live. Say it aloud until it feels natural, then try it with a real person.
