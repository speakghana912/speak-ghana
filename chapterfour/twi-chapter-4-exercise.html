Exercise 2: Say Your Age in Twi

Write and say these age sentences in Twi.

  1. I am [your age] years old. → ______________
  2. My brother/sister is [their age]. → ______________
  3. My father is [his age]. → ______________
  4. My mother is [her age]. → ______________
Pattern: Mewɔ mfeɛ [number]. / [Person] wɔ mfeɛ [number].
To ask: Woadi mfeɛ sɛn? (How old are you?)

Exercise 3: Tell the Time

Write what time it is in Twi.

  1. 3:00 → ______________
  2. 4:30 → ______________
  3. 5:15 → ______________
  4. 6:45 → ______________
  5. 7:20 → ______________
  6. 8:50 → ______________
1. Abɔ nnɔmmiɛnsa.  |  2. Abɔ nnɔnnan ne fa.  |  3. Nnɔnnum abɔ apa ho simma dunnum.  |  4. Aka simma dunnum na nnɔnson abɔ.  |  5. Nnɔnson abɔ apa ho simma aduonu.  |  6. Aka simma du na nnɔnwɔtwe abɔ.

Exercise 4: Days of the Week

Answer in complete Twi sentences.

  1. Ɛnnɛ yɛ da bɛn? (Look at a calendar!) → ______________
  2. Ɔkyena yɛ da bɛn? → ______________
  3. Nnora yɛ da bɛn? → ______________
  4. Which day do you like best? → ______________
  5. What do you do on Saturday? → ______________
Pattern: Ɛnnɛ yɛ [day].  |  Mepɛ [day].  |  Memeneda, me [verb]...

Exercise 5: Day Names — Kradin

What is the day name for each person? Write the Twi name (male or female) and the day they were born.

  1. A man named Yaw was born on → ______________
  2. A woman named Akosua was born on → ______________
  3. A man born on Wednesday is named → ______________
  4. A woman born on Friday is named → ______________
  5. What is your Akan day name? → ______________
1. Yawoada (Thursday)  |  2. Kwasiada (Sunday)  |  3. Kwaku  |  4. Afia / Efua  |  5. Depends on your own birthday — check Section D!

Exercise 6: Family Tree

Draw your family tree on paper, then write 5 sentences about your family members in Twi.

Patterns to use:
[Person] yɛ [occupation].  |  [Person] te [place].  |  Mewɔ [number] nuanom.  |  [Person] wɔ mfeɛ [number].
