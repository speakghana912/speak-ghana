Chapter 6 · Getting Around Nkran — 6 Real Travel Dialogues

Role-play time! 🚌

Six dialogues covering real Accra travel situations. Read both parts aloud, then switch roles. These are the conversations you will actually have on the streets of Nkran.

1

Tro-tro Bisa — Wɔ Circle

Asking for Tro-tro Directions at Circle
A: Mepa wo kyɛw, tro-tro a ɛkɔ Osu wɔ he?Please, where is the tro-tro going to Osu?
B: Hwɛ, fa ha so kɔ anim kakra. Bere a wobɛhunu traffic light no, fa nifa. Osu tro-tro wɔ hɔ.Look, go straight here a little. When you see the traffic light, turn right. The Osu tro-tro is there.
A: Ɛyɛ akyirikyiri?Is it far?
B: Daabi, ɛbɛn ha. Nantɛ ketewa bi.No, it's near here. Walk a little.
A: Ɛyɛ sɛn?How much is it?
B: Sika nwɔtwe.8 cedis.
A: Medaase paa!Thank you very much!
B: Yɛnda aseɛ. Nante yie!It is my pleasure. Walk well!
2

Taxi / Dropping — Kɔ Lapaz

Taking a Taxi to Lapaz
A: Maaha! Dropping?Good afternoon! Shared taxi?
Driver: Yiw! Wokɔ he?Yes! Where are you going?
A: Mekɔ Lapaz. Ɛyɛ sɛn?I'm going to Lapaz. How much?
Driver: Sika aduonum.50 cedis.
A: Aaa, ɛyɛ dɔɔ so. Yɛ no aduasa?Aaa, it's too expensive. Make it 30?
Driver: Mepa wo kyɛw, aduanan.Please, 40 cedis.
A: Yoo, aduanan. Mekɔ.OK, 40. I'm going.
Driver: Foro mu.Get in.
— Later, arriving at Lapaz —
A: Gyina ha! Me ba ha. Fa sika aduanan.Stop here! I'm getting off here. Take 40 cedis.
Driver: Medaase! Nante yie!Thank you! Walk well!
3

Kwan Bisa — Stranger Helps

Asking a Stranger for Directions to 37 Hospital
A: Maakye! Mepa wo kyɛw, ɛhe na 37 wɔ?Good morning! Please, where is 37 (Hospital)?
B: Maakye! Wopɛ 37? Ɛwɔ hɔ a ɛbɛn.Good morning! You want 37? It's near there.
A: Ɛkwan bɛn na mefa so?Which road do I take?
B: Kɔ wo anim kɔsi sɛ wobɛhunu traffic light kɛse no. Fa nifa. Ɛyɛ nantɛ sima nnum pɛ.Go straight until you see the big traffic light. Turn right. It's just a five-minute walk.
A: Ɛbɛn ha?Is it near here?
B: Yiw, ɛbɛn paa. Wobɛhunu no wɔ wo nifa so.Yes, very near. You'll see it on your right side.
A: Mepa wo kyɛw, ka no bio nkakrankakra. Mente ase pa ara.Please, say it again slowly. I don't understand very well.
B: Yoo. Kɔ wo anim. Traffic light no, fa nifa. 37 wɔ wo nifa so.OK. Go straight. At the traffic light, turn right. 37 is on your right.
A: Medaase paa! Woadi me ɔkye!Thank you very much! You have saved me!
B: Yɛnda aseɛ. Nante yie!It is my pleasure. Walk well!
4

Wɔ Tro-tro Mu

On the Tro-tro — Boarding and Getting Off
Mate: Osu! Osu! Osu! Foro mu!Osu! Osu! Osu! Get on!
A: Wokɔ Osu? Ɛyɛ sɛn?Are you going to Osu? How much?
Mate: Sika du. Tua ha.10 cedis. Pay here.
A: Fa sika du. Medaase.Take 10 cedis. Thank you.
Mate: Mia kakra! Mia kakra!Push a little! Push a little!
— Approaching Osu —
A: Me ba ha!I'm getting off here!
Mate: Osu? Yoo, gyina. Sian ha.Osu? OK, stop. Get off here.
A: Medaase!Thank you!
Mate: Yɛnda aseɛ!It is my pleasure!
5

Uber / Bolt — Kɔ Airport

Talking to your Uber/Bolt Driver in Twi
Driver: Maaha! Wone [dzina]?Good afternoon! Are you [name]?
A: Yiw, me ne no.Yes, that's me.
Driver: Wokɔ he?Where are you going?
A: Mekɔ Airport Residential. Wonim hɔ?I'm going to Airport Residential. Do you know it?
Driver: Yiw, minim. Ɛbɛn airport no.Yes, I know it. It's near the airport.
A: Ɛyɛ. Traffic wɔ?Good. Is there traffic?
Driver: Traffic wɔ kakra, nanso yɛbɛdu ntɛm ara.There's a little traffic, but we'll arrive soon.
— Arriving —
Driver: Yɛadu. Ha?We've arrived. Here?
A: Yiw, ha ara. Medaase paa!Yes, right here. Thank you very much!
Driver: Yɛnda aseɛ. Nante yie!It is my pleasure. Walk well!
6

Yera Kwan — Getting Lost

Lost near Makola — Asking for Help
A: Mepa wo kyɛw, me ayera kwan. Mehwehwɛ Makola.Please, I am lost. I am looking for Makola.
B: Makola? Ɛwɔ ha a ɛbɛn. Wofiri he?Makola? It's near here. Where are you coming from?
A: Mefiri Circle. Mesian tro-tro, nanso menyim kwan.I came from Circle. I got off the tro-tro, but I don't know the way.
B: Hwɛ, fa saa ɔkwan kɛse yi kɔ wo anim. Bere a wobɛhunu asɔre kɛse no, kɔ benkum. Makola wɔ hɔ.Look, take this big road straight ahead. When you see the big church, turn left. Makola is there.
A: Kɔ benkum wɔ asɔre no?Turn left at the church?
B: Yiw. Wobɛhunu no — ɛyɛ kɛse paa.Yes. You'll see it — it's very big.
A: Ɛyɛ akyirikyiri?Is it far?
B: Daabi, sima nnum pɛ.No, just five minutes.
A: Medaase paa! Woadi me ɔkye.Thank you very much! You have saved me.
B: Yɛnda aseɛ. Nante yie! Na sɛ woyera kwan bio a, bisa obi!It is my pleasure. Walk well! And if you get lost again, ask someone!
Practice tip: Pick Dialogue 2 or 3 and change the destination to somewhere you actually know. Practice bargaining in Dialogue 2 until the price feels automatic. For Dialogue 6, try starting lost in a different part of Nkran.
