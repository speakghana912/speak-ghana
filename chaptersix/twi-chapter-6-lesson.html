Akenkan — Reading Passage

Kofi Yera Kwan Wɔ Accra — Kofi Gets Lost in Accra

The streets of Accra — tro-tros, taxis, and people everywhere

Kofi yɛ ɔbarima foforo wɔ Nkran. Ɔfiri Kumasi, nanso nnɛ na ɔkɔ Nkran mmere a ɛdi kan. Ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ Makola edwam, nanso ɔnnim kwan no pa ara. Kofi is a young man new to Accra. He is from Kumasi, but today he is going to Accra (Nkran) for the first time. He wants to go to Makola market, but he does not know the way at all.

Ɔsii wɔ Circle, ɔhwɛɛ nipa pii wɔ edwam no mu. Tro-tro pii rekɔ baabi; mate pii refrɛ: "Osu! Osu!" "Kaneshie! Kaneshie!" "Lapaz! Lapaz!" Kofi kaa sɛ: "Makola? Makola wɔ he?" He got off at Circle and looked at many people in the station. Many tro-tros were going everywhere; many mates were calling: "Osu! Osu!" "Kaneshie! Kaneshie!" "Lapaz! Lapaz!" Kofi said: "Makola? Where is Makola?"

Ɔbaa baako huu no na ɔpenee no: "Maakye! Wokɔ Makola?" Kofi tiemm sɛ: "Yiw, mepɛ Makola, nanso me ayera kwan!" Ɔbaa no sereeɛ no na ɔkaa sɛ: "Makola bɛn ha. Kɔ anim kakra kɔsi sɛ wobɛhunu traffic light kɛse no. Fa nifa. Wobɛhunu Makola wɔ wo nifa so." A woman saw him and greeted him: "Good morning! Do you want Makola?" Kofi exclaimed: "Yes, I want Makola, but I am lost!" The woman smiled and said: "Makola is near here. Go straight until you see the big traffic light. Turn right. You will see Makola on your right side."

Kofi dwenee kakra na ɔkaa sɛ: "Mepa wo kyɛw, ka no bio nkakrankakra. Mente ase pa ara." Ɔbaa no seree na ɔkaa sɛ: "Kɔ wo anim. Wobɛhunu traffic light no, fa nifa. Makola wɔ hɔ." Kofi ani gyeɛ na ɔka sɛ: "Ɛyɛ, mate aseɛ. Medaase paa!" Kofi thought for a moment and said: "Please, say it again slowly (nkakrankakra). I don't understand very well." The woman smiled and said: "Go straight (Kɔ wo anim). When you see the traffic light, turn right. Makola is there." Kofi understood and said: "OK, I understand. Thank you very much!"

Ɔbaa no kaa sɛ: "Yɛnda aseɛ. Nante yie! Na sɛ woyera kwan bio a, bisa obi!" Kofi sereeɛ na ɔkɔɔ anim. Ɔduu Makola sima sɛn? Sima nnum pɛ! Ɔkaa sɛ: "Accra yɛ kɛse, nanso nipa no yɛ pa!" The woman said: "It is my pleasure. Walk well! And if you get lost again, ask someone!" Kofi smiled and went forward. How long did it take him to reach Makola? Just five minutes (sima nnum pɛ)! He said: "Accra is big, but the people are good!"

🔄 Put in Order

The woman gave Kofi 3 direction steps. Put them in the correct order:

Fa nifa. Kɔ anim so. Wobɛhunu Makola wɔ wo nifa so.
Answer
1️⃣ Kɔ anim so. — Go straight.
2️⃣ Fa nifa. — Turn right (at the traffic light).
3️⃣ Wobɛhunu Makola wɔ wo nifa so. — You will see Makola on your right.

  2. Ɔbaa no huu Kofi na ɔpenee no. ___
  3. Kofi te aseɛ kan. ___

Answers

🧮 Work It Out

Kofi asked the woman to repeat the directions. What two Twi phrases did he use — one to say he doesn't understand, one to ask her to speak slowly?

Answer
Mente ase pa ara. — I don't understand very well.
Ka no bio nwaanwaa. — Say it again slowly.
🧠 Think Beyond

The woman ended by saying "Sɛ woyera kwan bio a, bisa obi!" Why is this good advice for anyone new to Accra? What does it tell you about Ghanaian culture?

Discussion points
• In Ghana, asking strangers for help is completely normal and expected — people are generally willing to help.
• Street addresses barely exist in Accra — directions always involve asking people and using landmarks.
• The phrase reflects the communal culture of Ghana: you are never truly alone on the road.

Akwantuo Mmoa — Transport in Accra

Types of transport, tro-tro vocabulary, taxi phrases

A tro-tro station — mates calling destinations, passengers boarding

🚌

Tro-tro

Shared minibus — fixed routes, cheapest option. The backbone of Accra transport. Mates call destinations.

🚕

Taxi / Dropping

Shared (dropping) or private hire. Agree price before getting in. More expensive than tro-tro.

📱

Uber / Bolt

Ride-hailing apps — price set by app. Popular in Accra. Drivers may greet you in Twi!

🏍️

Okada

Motorbike taxi — fast for short distances. Always agree price first. Wear a helmet if provided.

🚶

Nantew — Walking

For short distances in neighbourhoods. Many places within a 5–10 minute walk of each other.

🚌

Bus — STC / VIP

Coaches for longer journeys to other cities. Tema Station for Eastern Region; STC for intercity.

Giving Directions — Using Landmarks

In Accra, people rarely use street names. They use landmarks. Always mention a landmark the person will recognise — a church, market, filling station, or major building.

PatternExample
Kɔ anim so kɔsi sɛ wohunu [landmark].Kɔ anim so kɔsi sɛ wohunu asɔre kɛse no.
Fa nifa / benkum wɔ [landmark].Fa nifa wɔ traffic light no.
Ɛwɔ [landmark] akyi no.Ɛwɔ bank no akyi no.
Ɛwɔ [landmark] animfie.Ɛwɔ ayaresabea no animfie.
Ɛbɛn [landmark] no.Ɛbɛn petrol gyappe no.
Wobɛhunu [place] wɔ wo [nifa/benkum] so.Wobɛhunu Makola wɔ wo nifa so.
