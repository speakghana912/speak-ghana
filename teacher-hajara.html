About Hajara

Hajara Hashim is a dedicated Hausa language educator with proven experience in teaching, translation, and community engagement. As a native Hausa speaker, she is skilled in delivering structured and conversational lessons to diverse learners, including non-native speakers. She is experienced in both physical and virtual learning environments, with a strong passion for language education and cultural exchange.

Hajara believes that language is the key to understanding culture and building meaningful connections. Her students consistently praise her patience, clarity, and ability to make complex concepts accessible to learners at any level.
