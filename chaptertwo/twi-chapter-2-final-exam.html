📖 Read First: "Wɔ Edwam" (At the Market)

Anɔpa bi, Akosua kɔɔ edwam.One morning, Akosua went to the market.
Ɔhyiaa ne yɔnko Kofi wɔ hɔ.She met her friend Kofi there.
Kofi kaa sɛ: "Maakye, Akosua! Wo ho te sɛn?"
Akosua buae sɛ: "Maakye, Kofi! Me ho yɛ, medaase. Wo nso ɛ?"
Kofi kaa sɛ: "Me ho nso yɛ. Wopɛ dɛn wɔ edwam ha?"I am fine too. What do you want here at the market?
Akosua kaa sɛ: "Mepɛ akonnwa mmienu ne abirekyie baako."I want two chairs and one goat.
Kofi serewee na ɔkaa sɛ: "Ɛyɛ! Wobɛtɔ akonnwa mmienu ne abirekyie baako wɔ ha."Nice! You will buy two chairs and one goat here.
Akosua kaa sɛ: "Medaase, Kofi. Yɛbɛhyia bio!"
Kofi buae sɛ: "Yoo, nante yie!"
