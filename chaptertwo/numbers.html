📊 Twi Numbers 1–20 (Counting People & Things)

When counting people, place the number after the noun: nipa baako (one person), nnipa du (ten people), nnipa aduonu (twenty people). For things, use the same numbers with the noun: akonnwa baako (one chair), mpaboa du (ten shoes).
