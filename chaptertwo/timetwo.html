⏰ Tell the Time in Twi

O'clocks · Clock Exercises · Practice

📘 Introduction

Having learned to count in Twi, and equipped yourself with time-telling vocabulary, let's now move on to see how to actually tell time in Twi. We begin with the o’clocks.

🔑 Key rule: The Twi term for ‘o’clock’ comes in two forms:
  • • dɔn (singular) — used only for 1 o’clock
  • • nnɔn (plural) — used for 2 o’clock through 12 o’clock

🕐 O’clocks in Twi

dɔn ko / dɔn koroone (1) o’clock
nnɔn mienutwo (2) o’clock
nnɔn miɛnsathree (3) o’clock
nnɔn nanfour (4) o’clock
nnɔn numfive (5) o’clock

nnɔn dumienutwelve (12) o’clock

💡 The emboldened parts (ko, mienu, miɛnsa, etc.) are the Twi numbers you've learned previously.

🗣️ Telling the Time (Full Sentences)

Use abɔ (“it has struck”) followed by the o’clock expression.

TwiEnglish
abɔ dɔn ko / dɔn koroit’s one (1) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn mienuit’s two (2) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn miɛnsait’s three (3) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn nanit’s four (4) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn numit’s five (5) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn siait’s six (6) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn sonit’s seven (7) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn wɔtweit’s eight (8) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn kronit’s nine (9) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn duit’s ten (10) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn dubaakoit’s eleven (11) o’clock
abɔ nnɔn dumienuit’s twelve (12) o’clock

🌅 Adding a.m./p.m. or Time of Day

Add the name of the time period before the o’clock:

TwiEnglish
abɔ awia dɔn koit’s 1 p.m.
abɔ anwummerɛ nnɔn siait’s 6 p.m.
abɔ anɔpa nnɔn sonit’s 7 a.m.
abɔ anɔpa nnɔn wɔtweit’s 8 a.m.
abɔ anadwo nnɔn duit’s 10 p.m.
prɛmo atoit’s midday / noon

❓ Asking for the Time

abɔ sɛn? — “What time is it?”
