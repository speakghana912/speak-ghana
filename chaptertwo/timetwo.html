A

Berɛ a Ɛyɛ · Introduction

Having learned to count in Twi, and equipped yourself with time-telling vocabulary, let's now move on to see how to actually tell time in Twi. We begin with the o'clock.

🔑 Key rule: The Twi term for 'o'clock' comes in two forms:
  • • dɔn (singular) — used only for 1 o'clock
  • • nnɔn (plural) — used for 2 o'clock through 12 o'clock

Grammar Corner · Time Expressions

Key patterns to remember:

  • abɔ + dɔn/nnɔn + number = "it's [number] o'clock"
  • Time period + abɔ + dɔn/nnɔn + number = "it's [number] [a.m./p.m.]"
  • abɔ sɛn? = "What time is it?"

Note: dɔn is singular (1 o'clock), nnɔn is plural (2-12 o'clock).
