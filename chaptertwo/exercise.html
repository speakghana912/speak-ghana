📘 Noun Categories Review (Edin Ahorow)

A noun is a word used to identify a person, place, thing, animal, or idea/quality/state.

👤 Person Kofi, Adwoa, ɔsɔfo, ɔkyerɛkyerɛni, ɔsraani, ɔtomfoɔ, Yaa, Boafo
📍 Place Amɛreka, aburokyire, ayaresabea, Sunyani, Berekum, Tafo, mmarahyɛ badwam
📦 Thing krataa, ahoma, toa, didipono, ntoma, mpaboa, kasafidie, dadeɛ, kyiniiɛ
🐘 Animal akokɔ, tɛfrɛ, odwan, kɔtɔ, dɛnkyɛm, ntontom, afunumu pɔnkɔ, ɔwɔ
