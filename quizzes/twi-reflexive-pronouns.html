🪞 Twi Reflexive Pronouns Lesson + Quiz

🎯 Reflexive pronouns show when the subject and object are the same (myself, yourself, himself, etc.)

🪞 What is a Reflexive Pronoun?

A reflexive pronoun is used when the subject and the object of a sentence refer to the same person, animal, or thing. It shows that the doer of the action is also the receiver of the action.

Medɔ me ho.
I love myself.
Wogye wo ho di.
You believe in yourself.
Ɔsiesiee ne ho.
He/She dressed himself/herself.
📝 How to Form Reflexive Pronouns in Twi

Reflexive pronouns in Twi are formed by adding ho (meaning "self") to the possessive adjectives.

📌 Formation Rule: Possessive Adjective + ho = Reflexive Pronoun
me (my) + ho = me ho
myself
wo (your) + ho = wo ho
yourself
ne (his/her) + ho = ne ho
himself/herself
💡 Important Note: Like possessive pronouns, Twi reflexive pronouns consist of two words each.

📌 Reflexive Pronouns as Objects
As Direct Objects: The entity directly affected by the verb.
Example: Meresere hwan? → Me ho. (Whom am I laughing at? → Myself.)
As Indirect Objects: The recipient of the direct object.
Example: Akosua bɔ mpaeɛ ma ne ho da biara. (Akosua prays for herself every day.)
