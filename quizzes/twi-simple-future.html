⏩ Twi Simple Future Tense Daakye Kabea

🎯 The simple future tense describes actions that will happen later (tomorrow, next week, etc.)

⏩ What is the Simple Future Tense?

The simple future tense is used to talk about actions or events that have not yet happened but are expected or likely to happen later (tomorrow, next week, in the future, etc.).

💡 In English: We usually add "will" before the verb.
💡 In Twi: We add bɛ- to the beginning of the verb.
📜 Main Rule

Rule: Add bɛ- to the beginning of the verb.

  • If the subject is a pronoun, combine it with the future marker + verb into one word.
  • If the subject is a noun, keep it separate from the verb.
📝 Examples
Wo bɛsoa.
You will carry.
Tweneboa bɛsom Nyame.
Tweneboa will worship God.
Wɔ bɛforo dua no.
They will climb the tree.
Nyame bɛyɛ.
God will do (it).
Ɔ bɛfa.
He/She will take.
⚠️ Special Case: First Person Singular ("Me" – I)

When the subject is me (I), a contraction occurs:

me + bɛ becomes mɛ

Mɛkɔ sukuu.
I will go to school.
Mɛda ntɛm.
I will sleep early.
Mɛsom wo.
I will worship/serve you.
Mɛnom nsuo.
I will drink water.
Mɛsa adowa.
I will dance Adowa.
📝 More Example Sentences
Ɔbɛba ɔkyena.
He/She will come tomorrow.
Yɛbɛtɔ fufuo.
We will buy fufu.
Mɛbɔ wo din.
I will call your name.
Kofi bɛtwa dua no.
Kofi will cut the tree.
