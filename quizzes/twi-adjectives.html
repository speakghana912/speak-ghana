📝 Twi Adjectives Lesson + Quiz

🎯 Adjectives describe nouns – size, color, shape, quantity, opinion (big, small, beautiful, tall, etc.)

📝 What are Adjectives?

Adjectives are words used to modify/describe nouns in sentences.

💡 English examples: tall, short, old, small, red, beautiful, smart, big, delicious
🏷️ Types of Twi Adjectives
TypeTwi ExamplesEnglish
Numericdu, aduonu, apemten, twenty, thousand
Quantitativebebree/pii, nyinaa, ɛfa, muamany/more, all, half, full
Qualitativekɔkɔɔ, tenten, kankanfair complexion, tall, smelly

🔢 Number Adjectives
Kwame ware mmaa du.
Kwame is married to ten women.
nka mewɔ tɛkyerɛma apem a, nka mɛyi Onyankopɔn ayɛ
If I had a thousand tongues, I would've praised God.
📊 Quantity Adjectives
nnipa pii ka Akan kasa
Many people speak the Akan language.
ɔmaa me kosua mua
He/She gave me a full egg.
Yeinom nyinaa yɛ ne dea
All these are his/hers.
🎨 Color Adjectives
sɛ wopɛ se fitaa a, twi
If you want white teeth, brush.
ɔkra tuntum no ayera
The black cat is missing.
mewɔ odwan fitaa
I have a white sheep.
📏 Size Adjectives
watwa dua kɛseɛ no
He/She has cut the big tree.
Akosua se ɔmpɛ barima tiatia
Akosua says she doesn't like a short man.

💭 Opinion Adjectives
okunu pa, hwan na ɔbɛnya
A good husband, who wouldn't want one?
Kofi anoa aduane dɛdɛɛdɛ bi
Kofi has cooked a delicious meal.
Aduane no yɛ dɛ
The food is delicious.
⚪ Shape Adjectives
adeɛ korokorowa bi da nsuo no mu
A rounded thing is lying in the water.
abaayewa feaa no didii Kofi atɛm
The lean girl insulted Kofi.
📍 Position of Adjectives in Twi
📌 Important Rule: Twi adjectives mostly appear AFTER the noun they describe (unlike English, where they come before).
aboa kɛseɛ no awu
The big animal has died (animal big DEF die-PAST)
ɔbaa yi ho yɛ fɛ
This woman is beautiful (woman this side is beautiful)
adaka no mu yɛ duru
The box is heavy (box DEF inside is heavy)
