🔤 Twi Affixes Prefixes & Suffixes (Nsianimu & Nsiakyire)

🎯 Affixes are letters added to the beginning (prefix) or end (suffix) of a word to change its meaning

🔤 What is an Affix?

An affix is a word element that can be attached to a root to create a new word.

Example: osuani (learner/disciple)
o-
Prefix
sua
Root (learn)
-ni
Suffix
Verb "sua" (learn) → Noun "osuani" (learner)
📌 Types of Affixes:
  • Prefix (Nsianimu) - Attached to the beginning of the root
  • Suffix (Nsiakyire) - Attached to the end of the root
🔄 Derivational vs Inflectional Affixes
Derivational Affixes

Create a completely new word with a different grammatical category.

sua (learn - verb) → osuani (learner - noun)
Inflectional Affixes

Modify words to indicate tense or grammatical function without changing word class.

su (cry - present) → suiɛ (cried - past)
📌 Prefixes (Nsianimu) - Nominalizing Prefixes

These prefixes turn a verb (action) into a noun (thing/person).

PrefixFunctionExample
ɔ- Used for people kyerɛ (to teach) → ɔkyerɛkyerɛni (teacher)
a- Used for objects or results of actions foro (to climb) → aforosie (ascent/climb)
e- / ɛ- Used for various objects bu (to break/harvest) → ɛbuo (nest/stone)
📌 Prefixes (Nsianimu) - Plural Prefixes

Prefixes change to show there is more than one of something.

PrefixFunctionExample
m- / n- Most common plural markers dan (house) → nnan (houses)
bayerɛ (yam) → mayerɛ (yams)
📌 Suffixes (Nsiakyire)

Suffixes are added to the end of a root word. They often define a person's profession, origin, or a state of being.

👤 Person Suffixes (-ni / -foɔ)
SuffixFunctionExample
-ni Singular - person of a certain profession or place Asante + -ni = Asanteni (Ashanti person)
-foɔ Plural/Collective - groups or professions ɔkua (farm) + -foɔ = ɔkuafoɔ (farmer)
📍 Locative/State Suffixes (-eɛ / -yɛ)
SuffixFunctionExample
-eɛ Indicates a place where an action happens gyina (to stand) → gyinaeɛ (bus stop)
-yɛ Indicates the act of doing something or a quality pɛ (to like) → pɛyɛ (liking/perfection)
