🔗 Twi Relative Pronouns Lesson + Quiz

🎯 Relative pronouns connect clauses and add information about nouns (who, which, that, whatever, whoever)

🔗 What is a Relative Pronoun?

A relative pronoun is a pronoun used to refer back to a noun or noun phrase that has already been mentioned in the sentence. It helps provide more information about that noun or to specify which one is being referred to.

💡 English examples: who, whom, which, that, whose, whoever, whomever, etc.
📌 Note: Do not confuse relative pronouns with interrogative (question) words.
🇬🇭 Relative Pronouns in Twi

In Twi, the function of relative pronouns is mostly handled by a single particle: a (pronounced áà — with a high-low tone).

📌 Rule: This particle a is placed after the noun or noun phrase it refers to.
📝 Examples
TwiEnglish
maame no a gya tɔɔ n'afuom no resu The woman whose farm caught fire is crying
Kofi waree ɔbaa a na ɔnhuu no da Kofi married a woman that he had never met
papa no a ɔbaa ha no atu kwan The man who came here has traveled
wokae berɛ a na wonni sika no? Do you remember the time when you didn't have money?
akwadaa no a ne nan mu abuo no kɔ ayaresabea The child whose leg is broken has gone to the hospital
aduro no a wode maa me no asa The medicine that you gave me is finished
merehwehwɛ obi a ɔyɛ kwadwofoɔ I'm looking for someone who is lazy
ɛdan no a yɛdaa mu nnora no adwiri agu The building in which we slept yesterday has collapsed
📝 Forming "Whoever", "Whatever", "Wherever"

To form these, take an indefinite pronoun and add the particle a.

Indefinite Pronoun+ aResultMeaning
obiara+ aobiara awhoever
biribiara+ abiribiara awhatever
baabiara+ abaabiara awherever
(e)biara+ abiara awhichever
📝 Examples with Whoever, Whatever, Wherever
Awurade nhyira wo, obiara a woboaa me no
God bless you, whoever helped me
ɛdan anaa kaa, ebiara a wopɛ no, mɛtɔ ama wo
A house or car, whichever you like, I'll buy it for you
wiase afɛɛfɛdeɛ yi nyinaa, biribiara a yɛamfa anwo yɛn no, yɛbɛwu agya hɔ
All these niceties of the world, whatever we weren't born with, we'll die and leave them behind
yi baako, obiara a w'ani gye ne ho
Choose one, whomever you like

✓ Twi uses the particle a as the main relative pronoun

✓ Add a to indefinite pronouns to form whoever, whatever, wherever
