📝 Twi Adverbs ƆKyerɛfoɔ

🎯 Adverbs modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs (how, where, when, how often, to what extent)

📝 What are Adverbs?

An adverb modifies a verb, an adjective, or another adverb. It gives more information about how, where, when, how often, to what extent, or how likely something happens.

💡 Twi adverbs are grouped into six main categories.
1️⃣ Manner Adverbs (Yɛbea Kyerɛfoɔ)

These tell how something is done.

TwiEnglish Translation
ɔgyee no anigyeɛ sohe/she received him/her/it happily
ɔkyerɛ adeɛ yiehe/she teaches well
Kwadwo kasa brɛooKwadwo speaks slowly
Yaa didi ahoɔhare soYaa eats quickly
2️⃣ Place Adverbs (Beaeɛ Kyerɛfoɔ)

These tell where something happens.

TwiEnglish Translation
ɛda soroit lies at the top
bra hacome here
kɔ hɔgo there
wɔredi agorɔ wɔ abɔntenthey are playing outside
3️⃣ Time Adverbs (Berɛ Kyerɛfoɔ)

These tell when something happens.

TwiEnglish Translation
Ama adidi dadaAma has eaten already
ɛnnora, mebaa haYesterday, I came here
mɛkɔ kurom ɔkyenaI will go to town tomorrow
yɛbɛhyia daakyewe will meet in the future/someday
me na mewiee kaneI was the one who finished first
4️⃣ Frequency Adverbs (Dodoɔ Kyerɛfoɔ)

These tell how often something happens.

TwiEnglish Translation
ɔntuu kwan dahe/she has never travelled
Kofi ntaa nyareKofi seldom falls ill
ɔhwee ase mprenuhe/she fell down twice
mahyia ɔmanpanyin Obama pɛnI have met President Obama before
Kofi asan aba bioKofi has come again
5️⃣ Degree Adverbs (Anoɔden Kyerɛfoɔ)

These tell to what extent or level something is done.

TwiEnglish Translation
ɛdan no mu yɛ sum dodothe room is too dark
Buruwaa kasa dodoBuruwaa talks too much
me ho yɛ pa araI am very well
6️⃣ Probability Adverbs (Akyinnyegyeɛ Kyerɛfoɔ)

These express how sure we are about something.

TwiEnglish Translation
ebia osuo no nnyae tɔ seeseiperhaps it won't stop raining now
ampa, wonim de!truly, you are knowledgeable!
sɛsɛɛ wadahe/she is probably sleeping by now
💡 Tip: Pay attention to adverbs you encounter in daily Twi conversations. They are very useful when forming sentences.
