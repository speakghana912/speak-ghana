🔊 The Vowels a, e, o in Twi

In Twi, the vowels a, e, and o can each be pronounced in two different ways depending on the word. Learning these sound pairs is essential for correct pronunciation!

💡 Important Note: The /æ/, /ɪ/, /ʊ/ variants are only realised when speaking and not written in standard Twi. When writing, these sounds are represented by the letters 'a', 'i', and 'o' respectively.
🔊 Vowel 'a' - Two Sounds

The vowel 'a' can be pronounced as /a/ (like 'a' in "BLACK") or as /æ/ (like 'a' in "ANIMAL").

/a/ sound
anomaa - bird
adaka - box
akokɔ - fowl
ankaa - orange
adanko - rabbit
/æ/ sound
barima /bærima/ - male/man
aduane /æduane/ - food
ani /æni/ - eye
baabi /bææbi/ - somewhere
aburoo /æburoo/ - maize
🔊 Vowel 'e' - Two Sounds

The vowel 'e' can be pronounced as /e/ (like 'e' in "EIGHT") or as /ɪ/ (like 'i' in "PIT").

/e/ sound
edin - name
esum - darkness
esie - (ant)hill
keteke - train
efie - home
/ɪ/ sound
ketewa /kɪtɪwa/ - small
tenten /tɪntɪn/ - tall/long
sensene /sɪnsɪnɪ/ - peel
sere /sɪrɪ/ - laugh
kenkan /kɪnkan/ - read
🔊 Vowel 'o' - Two Sounds

The vowel 'o' can be pronounced as /o/ (like 'o' in "POTENT") or as /ʊ/ (like 'oo' in "PUT").

/o/ sound
koko - porridge
afuo - farm
abufuo - anger
tuo - gun
aduro - medicine
/ʊ/ sound
soronko /sʊrʊnkʊ/ - unique
poma /pʊma/ - walking stick
adanko /adankʊ/ - rabbit
foro /fʊrʊ/ - climb
pono /pʊnʊ/ - door
📌 Key Takeaways
✓ The /æ/, /ɪ/, /ʊ/ variants are SPOKEN but not written in standard Twi.

✓ When writing, these sounds are represented by the letters 'a', 'i', and 'o'.

✓ The choice of sound variant follows Vowel Harmony rules.

✓ Practice these pairs daily to train your ear and mouth!
