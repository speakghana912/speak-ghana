⭐ Special Letters – Ɛ and Ɔ

These two letters are the most important to master because they change meaning completely!

🔊 Practice these pairs out loud:

• e vs ɛ: eye (eye) vs ɛyɛ (it is)
• o vs ɔ: obi? (someone) vs ɔdɔ (love)
