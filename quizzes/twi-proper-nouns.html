🏷️ Twi Proper Nouns Edin Pa

🎯 Proper nouns are specific names of people, places, days, and deities

🏷️ What are Proper Nouns (Edin Pa)?

Proper nouns refer to specific, unique names of people, places, days, and deities. Unlike common nouns, proper nouns are always specific to a particular entity and, in written Twi, begin with a capital letter.

💡 Examples: Kofi, Kumase, Ɛdwoda, Onyankopɔn
📂 Categories of Proper Nouns
CategoryTwi ExamplesEnglish Translation
Personal Names Kofi, Amma, Opoku, Antwi Specific names of people
Place Names Kumase, Nkran, Abibirem Kumasi, Accra, Africa
Days of the Week Ɛdwoda, Benada, Wukuada Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday
Deities/God Onyankopɔn, Tweaduampɔn God (The Supreme Being)
Months Ɔpɛpɔn, Ogyefuo, Ɔbɛnem January, February, March
📅 Day Names (Kradin) - Soul Names

Every Twi speaker has a name based on the day they were born. These are proper nouns that also reflect the "soul" (okra) of the person.

💡 Cultural Note: In Akan culture, your day name is considered your "soul name" and reflects your spiritual identity.
📍 Place Names: Suffixes "-man" and "-krom"

Many proper nouns for towns or countries are formed by adding suffixes to a root word.

-man (nation/town)
Asanteman (The Ashanti Nation), Ghanamantoɔ (The Republic of Ghana)
-krom (town/village)
Koforidua-krom, Kumase-krom (The city of Kumasi)
👥 Pluralizing Proper Nouns

Generally, proper nouns are singular because they refer to a unique entity. However, you can pluralize them if you are talking about a group of people with that name by adding the suffix -nom.

SingularPluralMeaning
KofiKofinomKofi and his group / The Kofis
AmmaAmmanomAmma and company
Wɔfa YawWɔfa YawnomUncle Yaw and those with him
