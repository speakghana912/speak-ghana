⏰ Twi Verb Tenses Present, Past & Future

🎯 Verb tenses tell us when an action happens - in the past, present, or future

⏰ What is a Verb Tense?

The tense of a verb is its form that indicates when the action it describes happens - whether it is in the past, present, or future.

💡 Three main verb tenses: Present Tense, Past Tense, Future Tense
📌 The Present Tense

The present tense is used to describe things that are currently happening or that are always the case.

TwiEnglish Translation
Menom nsuo.
📌 The Past Tense

The past tense is used to describe things that have already happened - events that took place before the current time of speaking.

TwiEnglish Translation
Asumadu too paanoo no.
📌 Past Tense Markers: Often use suffixes -eɛ or -yɛ on verbs (e.g., didi → didiiɛ)
📌 The Future Tense

The future tense is used to refer to things that have yet to happen - things that have not happened yet but are expected to happen in the future.

TwiEnglish Translation
Akua bɛware kyena.
📌 Future Tense Marker: bɛ- is added before the verb (e.g., bɛtɔ, bɛba)
