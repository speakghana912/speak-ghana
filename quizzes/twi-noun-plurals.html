👥 Twi Noun Plurals Part I: Person Nouns

🎯 Learn how to form plurals for person nouns in Twi (biako → bebree)

📚 Introduction

In Twi, forming plurals isn't as simple as adding an "s" like in English. Instead, we often change or add prefixes.

💡 Note: This lesson focuses on nouns that refer to people.
🔤 Rule 1: The "A-" Prefix

Many singular nouns that refer to people form their plural by adding or changing the prefix to a-.

Singular (Biako)Plural (Bebree)English Translation
ɔnuaanuanombrother/sister
ɔkuntakununomhusband
ɔyereayerenomwife
ɔbaa paamaa pagood woman
ɔdehyeɛadehyeɛroyal
🔊 Rule 2: The "N- / M-" Prefix

For several nouns, the plural is formed by adding a nasal prefix (n- or m-). The choice between n and m depends on the first letter of the root word.

Singular (Biako)Plural (Bebree)English Translation
ɔbarimammarimaman
ɔbaammaawoman
ɔkwadaankwadaachild
ɔpaninmpanyinelder
ɔkramannkramandog (often personified)
📌 Note: The nasal prefix changes based on the first letter of the word:
  • m- before b, p, m
  • n- before other consonants
👨‍🏫 Rule 3: Nouns Ending in "-foɔ" or "-ni"

Twi has specific suffixes for people belonging to groups, professions, or places. These have a very regular plural pattern.

📌 Pattern: -ni (Singular) → -foɔ (Plural)
Singular (Biako)Plural (Bebree)English Translation
ɔkyerɛkyerɛniakyerɛkyerɛfoɔteacher / teachers
ɔsɔfoasɔfoɔpriest / priests
ɔsraaniasraafoɔsoldier / soldiers
okuafoɔakuafoɔfarmer / farmers
oburoniaburofoɔwhite person / foreigners
bibiniabibifoɔblack person / Africans
🔄 Rule 4: Irregularities

Some family titles or specific person-nouns use different plural markers entirely, often adding -nom to the end.

Singular (Biako)Plural (Bebree)English Translation
ɛna (maame)ɛnanommothers
agya (papa)agyanomfathers
nuanuanomsiblings
korakoranomrival wives
