🚫⏩ What is the Negative Future Tense?

To form the negative future tense, use the negation marker "n-" or "m-" (as in the present) combined with the future marker bɛ-.

💡 The common pattern is: m-/n- + bɛ- → which often becomes mmɛ- or nnɛ- (with assimilation)
📜 Main Rules for Negative Future
Step 1: Start with the negation marker (m- / n-)
Step 2: Add the future marker bɛ- (assimilation occurs)
Step 3: The negative future marker often merges with the pronoun subject
Step 4: For 1st person singular (me), it commonly becomes mɛn- or menn-
