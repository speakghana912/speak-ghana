📦 Twi Common Nouns Edin Hunu

🎯 Common nouns are general names for people, places, things, animals, and ideas

📦 What are Common Nouns (Edin Hunu)?

Common nouns are words used to name general items, animals, or concepts rather than specific individuals or places. Unlike proper nouns, they are not capitalized unless they start a sentence.

💡 Examples: ɔkraman (dog), adaka (box), anigyeɛ (joy)
📂 Categories of Common Nouns
Living Things
  • ɔkraman - dog
  • dua - tree
  • abirekyie - goat
Household Objects
  • adaka - box
  • akonnwa - chair
  • pono - door
Natural Elements
  • nsuo - water
  • mframa - wind/air
  • egya - fire
Body Parts
  • ti - head
  • nsa - hand
  • nan - leg
Abstract Concepts
  • ahofadi - freedom
  • gyidi - faith
  • anigyeɛ - joy
🔤 Identifying Common Nouns by Prefixes

Most common nouns in Twi start with a vowel or a nasal prefix. These prefixes often help identify the noun's class or whether it is singular or plural.

📌 Vowel Prefixes: a-, ɛ-, ɔ-, e-
ɔdan (house), ɛmo (rice), apontuo (party)
📌 Nasal Prefixes: m-, n-
mpa (bed), nkyene (salt)
