🎯 Interrogative pronouns are question words (what, who, which, how, where)

❓ What are Interrogative Pronouns?

Interrogative pronouns are special pronouns used to ask questions.

💡 Important Note: Do not confuse interrogative pronouns with interrogative adjectives/determiners. The same question words can function as both, but they differ in use:
  • Interrogative adjectives modify nouns
  • Interrogative pronouns replace nouns and stand alone
📊 Interrogative Adjectives vs Pronouns
Interrogative AdjectivesInterrogative Pronouns
What car is this?What is this?
Which book did you read?Which did you read?
Kofi gave her what answer?Kofi gave her what?
