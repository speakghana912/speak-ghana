⏪🔄 Twi Past Continuous Tense Past Progressive

🎯 Describes actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past (I was going, she was eating)

⏪🔄 What is the Past Continuous Tense?

The Past Continuous (Past Progressive) describes actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past or actions that were ongoing when something else happened.

💡 English examples: I was going, She was eating, They were sleeping
📜 How to Form It
Formula: na + subject + re- + main verb
💡 This is the most common and natural way to express ongoing past actions.

📝 Examples in Sentences
Na merekɔ sukuu.
I was going to school.
Na wore nom nsuo.
You were drinking water.
Na ɔre di aduan.
He/She was eating food.
Na yɛre hwɛ TV.
We were watching TV.
Na more sa.
You (pl.) were dancing.
Na wɔre kɔ fie.
They were going home.
Na Kofi re noa fufu.
Kofi was cooking fufu.
Na Akosua re tɔ ntoma.
Akosua was buying cloth.
📝 Additional Examples (with interrupting action)
Na merekɔ sukuu a, na mehuu no.
While I was going to school, I saw him/her.
Na ɔre di aduan no, na ɔfrɛɛ me.
While he/she was eating, he/she called me.
📌 Key Notes
✓ na sets the action in the past.

✓ re- shows the action was ongoing (continuous).

✓ This tense is very useful for storytelling and describing what was happening at a particular moment.

✓ It is the standard way to express "was doing / were doing" in Twi.
