❓ Question Words in Twi (Nsɛmmisa)

In Twi (Akan), asking questions is essential for daily conversation. Here is the complete list of interrogatives with real examples. Note that question words can appear at the beginning OR at the end of sentences.

💡 Tip: Twi often uses intonation along with question words. Also, “na” can be used for emphasis: hwan na ɔbɛba? (Who will come?)
👤 hwan? — who? / whom? / whose?
Twi ExampleEnglish Translation
hwan na ɔdii aduane no?Who ate the food?
hwan na wohu?Whom do you see? / Who do you see?
ɛyɛ hwan adeɛ?Whose thing is it? (Whose property?)
hwan na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ?
Who wants to go?
❓ deɛn? — what?
Twi ExampleEnglish Translation
deɛn na woredi?What are you eating?
deɛn ne wo haw?What is your worry/problem?
yɛdeɛn?What should we do? / What is it?
🤔 adɛn? — why?
Twi ExampleEnglish Translation
adɛn na woresu?Why are you crying?
adɛn nti na wannya?Why didn't you get it?
adɛn enti na woadane?Why did you turn/change?
✨ Structure: “adɛn + na/enti” adds emphasis.
📍 ɛhe? — where?
Twi ExampleEnglish Translation
ɛhe na wote?Where do you live?
frɛm no ɛhe?Where is the hospital?
wofiri ɛhe?Where are you from?
🛠️ ɛkwan bɛn so? / sɛn? — how?
Twi ExampleEnglish Translation
ɛkwan bɛn so na wofaa so yɛe?How did you do it? (By which way?)
wo ho te sɛn?How are you? (lit. your body is how?)
wo din de sɛn?What is your name? (lit. your name is how?)
ɛte sɛn?
How is it? / How does it look?
⏰ berɛ bɛn? — when?
Twi ExampleEnglish Translation
berɛ bɛn na wowaree?When did you get married?
berɛ bɛn na wode bɛba?When will you bring it?
yɛbɛhyia berɛ bɛn?When shall we meet?
🔍 deɛhe? — which?
Twi ExampleEnglish Translation
deɛhe na wopɛ?Which one do you want?
deɛhe na eye sen?Which one is better?
📌 Note: “deɛhe” is often used to ask “which (one)” among options.
📖 Additional: Deɛn nti? — Why?

deɛn nti? also means “why?” (lit. what reason?). It's interchangeable with “adɛn” in many dialects. Example: deɛn nti na wankɔ? (Why didn't you go?)
