Nkɔmmɔdie ne Nsɛmmisa

Chapter 3 · Dialogues & Practice — Talking About Yourself and Your Daily Life

Role-play time!

Role-play — practice aloud with a friend, a family member, or by recording yourself.

These five dialogues use everything from this chapter — greetings, daily routine, food, family, and weekend plans. Read both parts out loud, then try switching roles.

1

Aduane ho nkɔmmɔ

Talking about Food
A: Wo ho te sɛn nnɛ?How are you today?
B: Nyame adom, me ho yɛ. Wo nso ɛɛ?By God's grace, I am fine. And you?
A: Me ho yɛ. Woredi deɛn awia yi?I am fine. What are you eating this afternoon?
B: Meredi banku ne nkwan. Wo nso ɛɛ?I'm eating banku and soup. And you?
A: Meredi fufuo. Ɛyɛ dɛ pa ara!I'm eating fufu. It's very delicious!
B: Ɛyɛ, me nso mepɛ fufuo. Yɛbɛdi bi daakye!Nice, I also like fufu. We'll eat some together another time!
2

Daadaa Dwumadie

Daily Routine
A: Wote he?Where do you live?
B: Mete Nkran, Osu. Na wo nso ɛɛ?I live in Accra, Osu. And you?
A: Mete Kumasi nanso mekɔ adwuma wɔ Nkran daa.I live in Kumasi but I go to work in Accra every day.
B: Woyɛ adwuma bɛn?What work do you do?
A: Meyɛ ɔkyerɛkyerɛni. Mekyerɛ mmofra Asante Twi.I am a teacher. I teach children Asante Twi.
B: Ɛyɛ papa! Na wo nso woyɛ dɛn?That's great! And what about you, what do you do?
A: Mesua Twi na mekɔ sukuu. Mepɛ sɛ mekasa yie.I am learning Twi and I go to school. I want to speak well.
3

Nea wopɛ ne nea wompɛ

Likes & Dislikes
A: Wopɛ deɛn aduane?What food do you like?
B: Mepɛ fufu ne nkwan pa ara! Na wo ɛɛ?I really like fufu and soup! And you?
A: Mepɛ banku, nanso menni dɔkono.I like banku, but I don't eat kenkey.
B: Adɛn nti na wompɛ dɔkono?Why don't you like kenkey?
A: Ne die yɛ den ma me, nanso mepɛ awaakye.Eating it is hard for me, but I like waakye.
B: Ɛyɛ, waakye yɛ dɛ! Yɛbɛdi bi ɔkyena?Nice, waakye is delicious! Shall we eat some tomorrow?
4

Abusuabɔ

Family
A: Wowɔ nuanom dodoɔ sɛn?How many siblings do you have?
B: Mewɔ nua barima baako ne nuammaa baanu. Na wo nso ɛɛ?I have one brother and two sisters. And you?
A: Mewɔ nua barima baanu. Wɔn nyinaa te Kumasi.I have two brothers. They all live in Kumasi.
B: Wo papa ne wo maame te he?Where do your father and mother live?
A: Me papa te Nkran, ɛna me maame te Oguaa.My father lives in Accra, and my mother lives in Cape Coast.
B: Ɛhɔ yɛ?Is that good / how is that?
A: Ɛyɛ kakra, mekɔ hwɛ wɔn daa.It's a bit much, I go to visit them often.
5

Nnawɔtwe Awieeɛ Nhyehyɛeɛ

Weekend Plans
A: Ɛdeɛn na woyɛ Memeneda?What are you doing on Saturday?
B: Memeneda, me taa da. Na wo nso ɛɛ?On Saturday, I usually sleep/rest. And you?
A: Me ne me nua kɔ biikyi. Yɛkɔgye yɛn ani.My sibling and I are going to the beach. We're going to enjoy ourselves.
B: Ɛyɛ anigye! Mokɔ biikyi bɛn?That's fun! Which beach are you going to?
A: Yɛkɔ Labadi. Wobɛba?We're going to Labadi. Will you come?
B: Daabi, mɛkɔ asɔre. Yɛbɛhyia anwummerɛ.No, I'm going to church. We'll meet in the evening.
🗣️ Practice idea: Pick one dialogue and act it out with a friend, your sibling, or your teacher. Then try changing the details — your own food, your own family, your own weekend plans — and say it again in your own words.
