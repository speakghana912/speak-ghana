Me din de Ama Tetebea. Meyɛ ɔkyerɛkyerɛni. My name is Ama Tetebea. I am a teacher.
Mekyerɛ adeɛ wɔ Suhum D/A Experimental C. I teach at Suhum D/A Experimental C.
Mekyerɛ brɔfo nanso mete Twi paa. I teach English but I understand Twi very well.
Anɔpa biara mesɔre ntɛm na masua adeɛ. Efirisɛ mempɛ sɛ me worɛ befiri bibiara. Every morning I wake up early and study. Because I do not want to be left behind by anyone.
Mewɔ sukuu nkwadaa aduonu wɔ me klasse. I have twenty students in my class.
Sɛ meba fie a, medi kan yi m'atadeɛ, mehome kakra na afei menoa me awia aduane. When I come home, I first take off my clothes, rest a little, and then cook my afternoon food.
Mepɛ ɛmo paa ara enti menoa anwummerɛ ɛmo ne nkosua. Mempɛ fufu ne banku. I love rice very much so I cook oil rice and eggs in the evening. I do not like fufu or banku.
Menni ba, menni kunu. Me nkoa na ɛte. I have no children, I have no husband. I live alone.
Anwummerɛ mekɔ asɔre. Mepɛ mpae bɔ paa ara. In the evening I go to church. I love to pray very much.
Mefiri asɔre ba efie a, mehwɛ kasanfonim kakra, mekɔ TikTok na afei mefrɛ me mpena. When I come home from church, I watch a little TV, go on TikTok, and then call my boyfriend.
Me kasa kakra pɛ na meda. Daakye me ne me mpena bɛware. I talk just a little then I sleep. In the future, my boyfriend and I will marry.
