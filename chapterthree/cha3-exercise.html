Exercise 1: Fill in the Correct Verb

Choose the right verb from the box and complete each sentence.

di  •  kɔ  •  te  •  pɛ  •  yɛ  •  sua
  1. Me ______ fufu daa. (eat)
  2. Wo ______ he? (live)
  3. Ɔ ______ adwuma anɔpa. (go)
  4. Me ______ Twi kakra. (learn)
  5. ______ dɛn nnɛ? (do)
  6. Me ______ banku paa. (like)
1. Me di fufu daa.
2. Wo te he?
3. Ɔ kɔ adwuma anɔpa.
4. Me sua Twi kakra.
5. Yɛ dɛn nnɛ?
6. Me pɛ banku paa.

Exercise 2: Make Sentences

Use the patterns you learned to write each sentence in Twi.

  1. I live in Accra. → ______________
  2. You go to school every day. → ______________
  3. He likes banku. → ______________
  4. We eat fufu. → ______________
  5. They don't eat kenkey. → ______________
  6. She speaks Twi. → ______________
1. Mete Accra.
2. Wokɔ sukuu da biara.
3. Ɔpɛ banku.
4. Yɛdi fufu.
5. Wɔnni dɔkono.
6. Ɔka Twi.

Exercise 3: Translate to Twi

  1. I eat fufu every day. → ______________
  2. Where do you live? → ______________
  3. What do you want? → ______________
  4. I don't understand. → ______________
  5. My brother goes to work. → ______________
  6. We will meet tomorrow. → ______________

2. Wote he?

Exercise 4: Make It Negative

Change these positive sentences to negative using n-.

  1. Medi fufu. → ______________
  2. Mekɔ sukuu. → ______________
  3. Mepɛ banku. → ______________
  4. Mete Accra. → ______________
  5. Yɛka Twi. → ______________
1. Menni fufu.
2. Menkɔ sukuu.
3. Mempɛ banku.
4. Mente Accra.
5. Yɛnka Twi.
