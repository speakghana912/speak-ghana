Chapter 5 · Market Dialogues — Real Shopping Conversations

Role-play time!

Practice these five dialogues aloud — with a partner, a family member, or by recording yourself.

Each dialogue covers a real shopping situation: a chop bar, Makola cloth market, buying produce, buying airtime, and buying pure water. Read both parts, then switch roles.

1

Wɔ Chop Bar

Buying Food at a Chop Bar
A: Maaha! Ɛte sɛn?Good afternoon! How is it?
B: Maaha! Me ho yɛ. Dɛn na wopɛ?Good afternoon! I am fine. What do you want?
A: Aduane wɔ hɔ?Is there food?
B: Ɛwɔ hɔ. Yɛwɔ fufu ne nkwan, banku ne okra.Yes. We have fufu and soup, banku and okra.
A: Mepɛ fufu ne nkwan. Ɛyɛ sɛn?I want fufu and soup. How much is it?
B: Ɛyɛ cedi aduasa.It is 30 cedis.
A: Boɔ no wɔ soro! Yɛ no cedi aduonu num?The price is high! Can you make it 25 cedis?
B: Mepa wo kyɛw, cedi aduonu nwɔtwe.Please, 28 cedis.
A: Yoo, mate aseɛ. Metua cedi aduonu nwɔtwe. Fa ma me.OK, I understand. I will pay 28 cedis. Give me (my food).
B: Fa w'aduane. Adane no yɛ dɛ!Take your food. The food is delicious!
A: Medaase paa!Thank you very much!
B: Yɛnda aseɛ. Bra bio!It is my pleasure. Come again!
2

Ntoma Tɔ — Wɔ Makola

Buying Cloth at Makola Market
A: Maakye! Wo ho te sɛn?Good morning! How are you?
B: Maakye! Me ho yɛ. Dɛn na wopɛ?Good morning! I am fine. What do you want?
A: Mehwehwɛ ntoma fɛfɛ.I am looking for beautiful cloth.
B: Hwɛ wei. Ɛyɛ fɛ pa ara.Look at this one. It is very beautiful.
A: Wei yɛ sɛn?How much is this?
B: Ɛyɛ cedi ɔha aduonum.It is 150 cedis.
A: Aaa! Boɔ no wɔ soro paa! Te boɔ no so kakra.Aaa! The price is very high! Reduce the price a little.
B: Yoo, cedi ɔha aduonu.OK, 120 cedis.
A: Ɛno ne nea metumi atua — cedi ɔha.That is all I can pay — 100 cedis.
B: Mepa wo kyɛw, cedi ɔha du.Please, 110 cedis.
A: Yoo, mate aseɛ. Metua cedi ɔha du.OK, agreed. I will pay 110 cedis.
B: Medaase! Fa wo ntoma.Thank you! Take your cloth.
A: Medaase paa! Nante yie!Thank you very much! Walk well!
3

Ntoosi ne Gyene Tɔ

Buying Tomatoes and Onions
A: Maaha! Wowɔ ntoosi?Good afternoon! Do you have tomatoes?
B: Yiw! Yɛwɔ ntoosi pa. Wopɛ sɛn?Yes! We have good tomatoes. How much do you want?
A: Mepɛ ntoosi kakra. Ɛyɛ sɛn?I want a little tomatoes. How much?
B: Ntoosi baako yɛ cedi ɛnum.One tomato is 5 cedis.
A: Mepɛ ntoosi ɛnum. Ɛyɛ sɛn?I want five tomatoes. How much?
B: Ntoosi ɛnum no, ɛyɛ cedi aduonu num.Five tomatoes is 25 cedis.
A: Mepɛ gyene nso. Wowɔ gyene?I also want onions. Do you have onions?
B: Yiw, gyene wɔ hɔ. Baako yɛ cedi ɛnan.Yes, onions are here. One is 4 cedis.
A: Ma me gyene mmienu. Ne nyinaa yɛ sɛn?Give me two onions. What is the total?
B: Ntoosi ɛnum ne gyene mmienu — cedi aduonu num ne nsia. Cedi aduonu baako nyinaa.5 tomatoes and 2 onions — 25 plus 8 cedis. Total is 33 cedis.
A: Yoo, fa cedi aduonu baako. Medaase!OK, take 33 cedis. Thank you!
B: Medaase! Bra bio!Thank you! Come again!
4

Airtime Tɔ

Buying Airtime from a Street Vendor
A: Maaha! Mepɛ airtime.Good afternoon! I want airtime.
B: Cedi sɛn?How many cedis?
A: Cedi aduonu.20 cedis.
B: Wopɛ MTN, Vodafone, ana AirtelTigo?Do you want MTN, Vodafone, or AirtelTigo?
A: MTN.MTN.
B: Fa cedi no. Hwɛ, w'airtime no aba.Give me the money. Look, your airtime has come.
A: Medaase! Ɛyɛ?Thank you! Is it correct?
B: Ɛyɛ. Nante yie!Yes it is. Walk well!
5

Nsuo ne Aburoo Tɔ

Buying Pure Water and Oranges
A: Maaha! Wowɔ nsuo?Good afternoon! Do you have water?
B: Yiw! Nsuo wɔ hɔ.Yes! Water is here.
A: Ɛyɛ sɛn?How much?
B: Baako yɛ pesewa aduonum.One is 50 pesewas.
A: Ma me nsuo mmienu.Give me two (bags of) water.
B: Ɛyɛ cedi baako.That is 1 cedi.
A: Wowɔ aburoo?Do you have oranges?
B: Yiw, aburoo wɔ hɔ. Wopɛ ahe?Yes, oranges are here. How many do you want?
A: Ma me aburoo mmiɛnsa. Ɛyɛ sɛn?Give me three oranges. How much?
B: Aburoo mmiɛnsa — cedi aduonu baako. Ne nsuo mmienu — cedi baako. Ne nyinaa cedi aduonu mmienu.3 oranges — 21 cedis. 2 waters — 1 cedi. Total is 22 cedis.
A: Fa cedi aduonu mmienu. Medaase!Take 22 cedis. Thank you!
B: Yɛnda aseɛ! Fa wo nneɛma.It is my pleasure! Take your things.
Practice: Pick Dialogue 2 or 3 and change the item and price to something you actually want to buy. Practice bargaining until you reach an agreed price — always stay polite, always smile.
