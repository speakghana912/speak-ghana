Exercise 9: What Does It Mean?

Imagine you hear these at the market. What do they mean in English?

  1. "Ɛyɛ cedi aduonu num." → ______
  2. "Boɔ no wɔ soro! Yɛ no aduonu." → ______
  3. "Mepa wo kyɛw, fa ma me." → ______
  4. "Bra bio!" → ______
  5. "Fa firi me." → ______
1. It is 25 cedis.  |  2. The price is high! Make it 20 (cedis).  |  3. Please, give me (it).  |  4. Come again!  |  5. Give me on credit.
